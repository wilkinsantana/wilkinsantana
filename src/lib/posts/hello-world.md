# Hello, World — A New Chapter

Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I've been meaning to build a personal site for years. Something that felt *mine* — not a template, not a LinkedIn mirror, not a GitHub README dressed up in HTML. A real space where I could write, think out loud, and show work I'm proud of.

This is that space.

## Why now?

Honestly? Because I got tired of waiting for the right moment. The right moment doesn't come. You make it.

So I built this site with [SvelteKit](https://kit.svelte.dev), TailwindCSS, and a lot of intentionality about what I actually want to say. The design choices are deliberate: the typography, the dark palette, the slow animations. I wanted something that felt cinematic and calm — like a space where ideas could breathe.

## What I'll write about

A few things I care deeply about and plan to explore here:

- **The craft of software engineering** — not tutorials, but the *why* behind decisions. Architecture, tradeoffs, lessons learned the hard way.
- **Building products that matter** — what it looks like to ship things with intention, from first principles to production.
- **Creative technology** — the intersection of design, code, and storytelling. This is where I live.
- **Reflections** — things I've learned, questions I'm sitting with, ideas that won't leave me alone.

## Who I am

I'm Wilkin Santana — a creative technologist, engineer, and builder. I work at the intersection of technology, design, and human meaning. I believe in making things that feel *right*, not just things that work.

Beyond the code: I'm curious about systems, aesthetics, language, and the hidden structures that shape how we experience the world.

This site is part portfolio, part journal, part experiment.
